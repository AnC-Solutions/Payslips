A&C Solutions Payslip Generator

Extract ZIP and open index.html in Chrome/Edge. Internet is needed for the Excel library. Download and fill the employee template, upload it, select an employee, choose month and enter gross salary only. Download payslip Excel, export payroll Excel, or print/save PDF.

Rules implemented: Basic 50%, HRA 20%, Other Allowance 30%; PF each share 12% of Basic. Gross above Rs.12,000: employee PF deducted and employer pays its share. Gross at/below Rs.12,000: employer bears both shares and employee PF deduction is zero. PT defaults to Rs.200 as an editable placeholder; confirm Telangana slab and applicable statutory payroll requirements before live use. Browser-local prototype; employee data is not uploaded to a server.
