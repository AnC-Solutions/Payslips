Configured company calculation: Basic = 50% of original salary; HRA = 20%; Other Allowance = 30%; each PF share = 12% of Basic. For salary ≤ ₹12,000, employee PF is added to gross and combined PF plus PT are deducted. For salary above ₹12,000, PT is added to gross and combined PF plus PT are deducted. Verify statutory payroll treatment before production use.

When saving as PDF, turn off Headers and footers in the browser print settings to remove the date, time and page title.
